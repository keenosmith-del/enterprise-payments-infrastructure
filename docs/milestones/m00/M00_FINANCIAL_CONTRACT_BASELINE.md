# M00 financial contracts and control examples

Revision 0.1 | 26 November 2026 | EPI 0.1.0 | Proposal for finance and maintainer approval.

## Values, identities and transaction controls

The initial slice accepts ZAR only. The wire contract represents minor units as a canonical unsigned decimal string, with an explicit currency and direction. This avoids JavaScript number loss above 2^53. Persist as signed 64-bit integer with amount bounds and a separate currency; sum using a widened exact representation and check bounds before persistence. Reject fractional minor units, signs and non-canonical forms at intake. Calculate rates using Decimal, retain rate and rule version, and propose half-even rounding once per bill line when converting calculated amounts to cents. Assert that rounded lines sum to the recorded total. Allocation, journal and settlement amounts do not undergo monetary rounding. Persist UTC occurrence and observation times separately from zoned business dates, value dates and accounting periods.

An obligation key is issuer, tenant, legal entity and stable obligation reference, with a separate immutable revision or adjustment reference. The Hub's instruction key joins tenant, legal entity, source system and stable source instruction ID. Store the material payload hash with canonicalisation version, including obligation, amount, direction, authority or approved payable reference, channel constraints and currency. Same key with same material hash returns the original receipt. A changed material payload under that key conflicts. An attempt has an identifier and sequence under the instruction. Allocate a separate provider operation ID before submission and persist it with a stable request digest. Store the provider account or rail namespace with the ID. Recover a leased attempt using the original ID. Store observed provider events keyed by their authoritative source and immutable revision.

The Hub records mandate reference, version, effective times, channel, limits and authentication evidence. The PAS owns the claimant approval and approved payable. Check authority again at dispatch, including amount, payee version, approved window, tenant, distinct maker/checker identity and revocation. Reserve the eligible balance for unknown execution; do not issue a replacement until definitive evidence and independently valid authority permit it. Lock aggregates in deterministic order or use serializable transactions and bounded whole-transaction retry. Keep every irreversible external call outside retryable database transactions. Do not derive business time or idempotency from transport arrival order.

## Journal and allocation

The Hub journals facilitated activity on control accounts. The insurer journals its own receivable, payable and cash effects. The independent simulator owns synthetic account balances. Post with currency, legal entity, period, immutable source reference and source-purpose key. Lock the period and affected balances, validate authority and check total debit equals total credit. Commit the posting, business state and outbox together. Require a database-enforced posting interface and deny direct runtime changes to posted records. Closed period adjustments use the next authorised open period, retaining original value date and posting reference. No balancing plug is inferred from a variance.

Use provisional controls for collection in transit (CIT), collections facilitated (CF), collected settlement eligible for allocation (CS), applied collection control (AC) and attributable fee control (FC). Execution uses Dr CIT / Cr CF. Settlement clears CIT against CS and any evidenced FC. Allocation transfers CS to AC. These are control accounts with periodic reconciliation and do not represent Hub cash or customer funds. Keep final clearing and account signoff for M02/M10. Allocation reference identifies the match, eligible balance reservation, obligation and source settlement items with rule version. A passive PAS delivery attempt cannot be counted as an applied policy receipt. Reverse by a new linked equal-and-opposite posting and independently deliver a compensating result.

## Worked expectations

Amounts below are integer ZAR cents. These are independent design fixtures for future tests.

1. Successful premium collection. Bill 75,000. Confirm provider execution and post Dr CIT 75,000 / Cr CF 75,000. Match settlement of 75,000 and post Dr CS / Cr CIT. Allocate 75,000 using Dr AC / Cr CS and send a single allocation event. PAS receivable is zero only after PAS applies the event.
2. Unsuccessful collection. Bill 75,000. Definitive unpaid evidence releases the 75,000 reservation and creates no execution or settlement posting. PAS remains due 75,000 and retains independent grace decisions.
3. Partial payment. Bill 75,000. Execution and settlement are 50,000 and each balanced posting uses 50,000. Allocate 50,000. PAS outstanding is 25,000 and CS remaining on the receipt is zero.
4. Delayed settlement. Independent execution is 75,000. CIT remains 75,000 while settlement is pending, with zero eligible allocation. Later independent gross 75,000, fee 500 and net 74,500 prove Dr CS 74,500 + Dr FC 500 / Cr CIT 75,000. Approval is required for gross discharge treatment. Under a provisional net-only policy, allocate 74,500, leave obligation residual 500 visible and open a fee treatment case. Do not post guessed fees.
5. Duplicate instruction. Deliver the same 75,000 instruction through API and SFTP. Return the original Hub receipt and create at most one eligible attempt. A changed amount 75,001 with original source identity conflicts. Expect no second posting, operation or reservation.
6. Lost acknowledgement. Provider commits 75,000 then the response is lost. Hold reservation and leave outcome unknown. Enquire using identical operation ID. If executed, post Dr CIT / Cr CF once. If the enquiry is unavailable, maintain the hold and create no additional provider operation.
7. Reversal. A fully allocated 75,000 collection returns in an open adjustment period. Reverse allocation Dr CS / Cr AC, settlement Dr CIT / Cr CS, and execution Dr CF / Cr CIT as independently authorised and evidenced, each with a linked 75,000 posting. PAS consumes a reversal of 75,000 and retains original applied history. If refund fees or other amounts differ, use explicit evidence and a separate correction.
8. Unmatched settlement. Receive 40,000 without eligible references. Retain unmatched evidence and an exception. Allocate zero and do not clear existing CIT against coincidence of amount.
9. Payroll discrepancy. Approved deductions expected 100,000. Employer reports 90,000 deducted and 10,000 rejected, while independently confirmed remittance is 85,000. Track 5,000 deducted but unremitted and 10,000 undeducted. Allocate no more than 85,000 and only to deterministically matched employee items. Quarantine ambiguous aggregate differences without reducing any policy balance by an unsupported amount.
10. Approved claim disbursement. PAS approves 125,000 and provides a stable beneficiary version and approval. Maker/checker releases one synthetic credit. Independent execution posts Dr disbursement in transit 125,000 / Cr disbursement facilitated 125,000. Independent settlement posts Dr disbursement settled 125,000 / Cr transit 125,000. PAS closes its own payable after authorised result consumption. Timeout leaves the execution reserved and unknown.

## Approval dependencies

Finance owns rounding policy, fee allocation, control chart, accounting dates, adjustment privileges and default handling when settlement and execution disagree. Platform owns contention, period locking and transaction retry proof. PAS and Hub owners approve receipt semantics and allocation idempotency. Simulator owner must demonstrate provider and payroll evidence comes from its own committed records. Review against FI-01 to FI-11, FR-003/010/011/015/018-025, SC-01 to SC-07 and NFR-001/002/003/007 before the relevant implementation.
